LaVerne Chief of Staff

An internal tool of LaVerne & Son

LaVerne Chief of Staff is a private, internal business-intelligence application built by and for LaVerne & Son. It is not a public product, has no external or customer-facing accounts, and is not offered for use by anyone outside LaVerne & Son.

The application reads operational and financial data from systems LaVerne & Son already uses to run its business — including QuickBooks Online and ServiceTitan — and consolidates that information into internal reporting used by LaVerne & Son's own management and staff.

Its connection to QuickBooks Online is read-only: it reads profit & loss, balance sheet, accounts receivable, customer, invoice, and payment data in order to reconcile it against LaVerne & Son's other internal systems. It never creates, edits, deletes, sends, or posts any QuickBooks Online record.

This page exists solely to satisfy Intuit's app-registration requirements for a public host domain, EULA, and privacy policy. It contains no company financial data, customer data, employee data, credentials, or source code.
