QuickBooks Online has been disconnected

Informational page

This page is shown to confirm that LaVerne Chief of Staff's access to a QuickBooks Online company has been disconnected.

Once disconnected, this application can no longer read any data from that QuickBooks Online company. No further QuickBooks Online reads occur until an authorized LaVerne & Son administrator reconnects it.

Was this intentional?

If an authorized LaVerne & Son administrator disconnected this application on purpose — for example, from QuickBooks Online's own "Manage connected apps" settings — no further action is needed.

If this disconnection was not intentional, see reconnecting QuickBooks Online for how an authorized administrator can restore the connection.

What this does not affect

Disconnecting this application does not delete or change any data already recorded in QuickBooks Online, and does not affect any other application connected to that QuickBooks Online company.
