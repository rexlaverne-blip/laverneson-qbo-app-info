Connecting QuickBooks Online

Informational page — no sign-in happens here

LaVerne Chief of Staff is a private, internal application used only by LaVerne & Son. There is no public sign-in flow: it does not have a hosted web login page, and it never asks anyone outside LaVerne & Son's own authorized personnel to connect a QuickBooks Online company.

This page is informational only. It does not initiate, store, or handle any QuickBooks Online sign-in, token, or credential. All authorization happens directly between an authorized LaVerne & Son administrator and Intuit's own QuickBooks Online sign-in flow, from LaVerne & Son's internal automation tooling.

How the connection works

An authorized LaVerne & Son administrator connects (or reconnects) this application's access to QuickBooks Online directly through Intuit's standard OAuth 2.0 authorization flow, initiated from LaVerne & Son's own internal automation tooling. The administrator signs in with their own QuickBooks Online credentials and approves the connection directly with Intuit — this application never sees or stores that password.

Reconnecting

If access is ever disconnected (see the disconnection page), an authorized LaVerne & Son administrator can reconnect it at any time by repeating the same authorization step through QuickBooks Online.

Scope of access

When connected, this application reads QuickBooks Online data only — profit and loss, balance sheet, accounts receivable aging, customers, invoices, and payments — for internal reconciliation and reporting. It does not create, edit, delete, send, or post anything in QuickBooks Online. See the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy for full detail.
