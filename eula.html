1. What this application is

LaVerne Chief of Staff is a private, internal business-intelligence and operations application built by and for LaVerne & Son. It is not a publicly distributed product, and it is not offered for use by, or marketed to, any person or organization outside LaVerne & Son. There are no public user accounts, no signup flow, and no commercial or consumer-facing features.

The application reads operational and financial data from systems LaVerne & Son already uses in the course of running its business — including QuickBooks Online, ServiceTitan, and other internal tools — and consolidates that information into internal dashboards and reports (the "Chief of Staff Cockpit") used by LaVerne & Son's own management and staff to run daily operations.

2. Who may use this application

Use of this application is restricted to authorized LaVerne & Son personnel and the internal systems LaVerne & Son operates on its own infrastructure. It is not licensed, sublicensed, or made available to any third party, customer, vendor, or member of the public.

3. QuickBooks Online access

This application connects to LaVerne & Son's own QuickBooks Online company file for the sole purpose of reading financial data already recorded there, including profit and loss figures, balance sheet figures, accounts receivable aging, customers, invoices, and payments. This connection is used to reconcile that data against LaVerne & Son's other internal systems (such as ServiceTitan) and to surface it in internal reporting.

In addition, and only after LaVerne & Son's owner has reviewed and explicitly approved each batch, this application may create vendor bills (accounts payable) and vendor credits in LaVerne & Son's own QuickBooks Online company, attach the vendor's invoice document (PDF) to the bill it created, and record the bill payments the owner has already made through the vendors' own payment portals. No bill, credit, or payment record is created without that prior owner approval. The application never moves money.

This application does not create, modify, delete, send, or post any other kind of transaction or record inside QuickBooks Online, such as customer invoices, journal entries, or customer records. Any additional write capability would require a change to this agreement and to LaVerne & Son's governance rules.

It is not sold, licensed, or otherwise provided to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or marketing purposes. See the Privacy Policy for further detail.

6. Disconnection

Any authorized LaVerne & Son administrator may disconnect this application's access to QuickBooks Online at any time via QuickBooks Online's own "Manage connected apps" settings, or by revoking the application's credentials at the integration layer. Disconnection immediately stops all further reads; no further action is required to make disconnection effective. See what happens after disconnection.
