Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-03 · Operator: LaVerne & Son · Contact: rexlaverne@laverneson.com

1. Scope

This policy describes how LaVerne & Son's internal "LaVerne Chief of Staff" application handles data obtained through its connection to QuickBooks Online. This application is private and internal to LaVerne & Son — it is not a public product, and this policy is not directed at consumers or the general public.

2. What data we access

Through a QuickBooks Online connection authorized by LaVerne & Son, this application reads:

  • Company profile information (company name, industry)
  • Profit and loss data
  • Balance sheet data
  • Accounts receivable aging data
  • Customer records (name, contact information, balance)
  • Invoices and payments

This access is read-only. The application does not create, edit, delete, send, or post any object in QuickBooks Online.

3. How we use this data

Data read from QuickBooks Online is used only to:

  • Populate internal operational and financial reporting for LaVerne & Son management (the "Chief of Staff Cockpit" and related internal reports)
  • Reconcile QuickBooks Online records against LaVerne & Son's other internal systems (such as ServiceTitan) to identify discrepancies, duplicates, or gaps in that reconciliation

We do not use this data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to LaVerne & Son's own internal business operations.

4. Where this data is stored

Data retrieved from QuickBooks Online is stored only in databases and systems that LaVerne & Son owns and directly controls. It is retained only as long as needed to support the internal reporting described above, and is not replicated to any third-party service beyond the systems LaVerne & Son already uses to run its business (e.g., its own database infrastructure).

5. Who can access this data

Access to this data is limited to authorized LaVerne & Son personnel and to the internal automated systems LaVerne & Son operates for this application. It is not shared with, or made accessible to, any customer, vendor, contractor, or member of the public.

6. Data we do not do

  • We do not sell customer or accounting data to any third party.
  • We do not use this data for advertising.
  • We do not share this data outside LaVerne & Son except where required by law.

7. Security

We take reasonable, practical steps to protect this data — including restricting access to authorized personnel and systems, and keeping the QuickBooks Online connection scoped to read-only use. This policy does not claim compliance with any specific security certification or regulatory framework, as none is currently in place.

8. Revoking access

An authorized LaVerne & Son administrator may revoke this application's access to QuickBooks Online at any time through QuickBooks Online's "Manage connected apps" settings. Once revoked, the application can no longer read any QuickBooks Online data. See what happens after disconnection.

9. Data deletion

Because this application retains QuickBooks-derived data only to support LaVerne & Son's own internal, ongoing reporting, deletion requests should be directed to rexlaverne@laverneson.com, and will be handled consistent with LaVerne & Son's operational need to retain accurate historical business records.

10. Changes to this policy

LaVerne & Son may update this policy as the application's internal use evolves. The current version is always the one accompanying the deployed application.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or this application's data handling may be directed to rexlaverne@laverneson.com.
